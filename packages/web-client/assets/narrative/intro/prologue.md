## Awakening of Mayhaps

You are Mayhaps, a sphere forged from the roulette heart of the cosmos. Every spin, every bounce, every shimmer of your shell is a wager.

The Lucky Architect deals in gravitational debts and luminous favors. They have invited you into the cosmic casino to prove that fortune can be sculpted.

Feel the velvet vacuum of the hall. Hear the orbiting slot machines. The stakes are expectation itself.
